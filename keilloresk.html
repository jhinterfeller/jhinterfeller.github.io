05 / The Back Porch

Hold My Beer.

Fiction, strange ideas, experiments and creative work. Separate from the travel journal, but part of the same mind.

Creative workshop

Not everything has to be useful.

This is where the rules loosen. Characters can talk to beetles. Mice can have names. A story can go somewhere real without being a travel report.

The important thing is that the main journal remains clear. Travel readers get travel. Fiction readers can come looking for the weird stuff.

Keilloresk can become its own audience. If a story works, it gets its own page. If it becomes a book, the page becomes the doorway.
